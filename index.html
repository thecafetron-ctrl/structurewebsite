008 QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Asked often.

What is Structure?+

Structure (Structure Logistics) is an AI for logistics company. We build autonomous AI systems that run freight operations end to end — quoting, dispatch, invoices, customs, forecasting and lead generation — and we make LoadHawk, the AI agent platform for US freight brokers.

What does "AI for logistics" actually mean?+

One intelligent system replaces the manual layer of freight. Rate requests are read and priced in minutes, dispatch steps coordinate themselves, invoices reconcile automatically, customs paperwork generates itself, and demand is forecast with up to 92% accuracy — 24/7, on every lane.

What is LoadHawk?+

LoadHawk is Structure's AI agent platform for freight brokers. Seven agents handle carrier outreach, rate negotiation against live DAT data, reliability scoring, board spotting, AI phone calls, 14-second shipper support and dormant-carrier reactivation — with a $30,000-in-90-days guarantee. Who is Structure built for?+

Freight brokers, freight forwarders and 3PLs. LoadHawk fits independent US brokerages moving 50+ loads a month with $1M–$10M in revenue. The Structure Platform serves logistics operators across the US and the Middle East.

Does the AI replace my team?+

No. Structure systems do the repetitive work — outreach, pricing, paperwork — and your people approve the decisions that matter. Every LoadHawk booking still requires dispatcher sign-off. Same team, dramatically more leverage.

What results should I expect?+

Across deployments: roughly 30% cost reduction, up to 92% forecast accuracy, 60% of team time returned and 99.7% uptime. LoadHawk cuts outreach from 45 minutes to under 10 per load and guarantees $30,000 in added revenue within 90 days — or a full refund.
